## 1. Goal

Build a connected set of Texas local-service pages that can earn relevant organic traffic and convert qualified bookkeeping, tax, payroll, and contractor-business leads.

The model is not “one thin page for every city.” It is a useful hierarchy:

```text
Texas service hub (/texas-tax-accounting-services)
|
|-- City hub: Dallas tax accountant
|   |-- Dallas contractor bookkeeping services
|   |-- Dallas small-business bookkeeping services
|   `-- Dallas payroll services for small businesses
|
|-- City hub: Fort Worth tax accountant
|   |-- Fort Worth catch-up bookkeeping for contractors
|   `-- Fort Worth small-business tax preparation
|
`-- City hub: Austin tax accountant
    `-- Austin bookkeeping for electricians
```

Every child page must answer a distinct search need, link to its city hub and related service hub, and contain information that genuinely helps that industry in that market.

## 2. Non-Negotiable SEO Rules

1. Assign one primary keyword and one primary intent to every URL.
2. Do not publish a city-name swap of another page. Each page needs unique examples, FAQs, workflow details, and internal links.
3. Do not claim an office, staff, license, client, result, or local partnership that IntegraFin cannot verify.
4. Do not use `CPA` in titles, headings, schema, or copy unless the applicable person or firm is licensed to use it.
5. Publish Indian-American, NRI, FBAR, FATCA, or US–India pages only after the firm confirms that it has the qualified expertise and review process to deliver those services.
6. Avoid promises of refunds, savings, audit outcomes, or penalty relief. Use accurate language such as “review,” “help organize,” and “support tax-ready records.”
7. Do not index a page until it has complete content, a canonical URL, internal links, metadata, and sitemap coverage.

## 3. How We Choose High-Opportunity Keywords

“High ranking” and “low competition” cannot be assumed from a keyword phrase. They must be validated before the page is approved.

### 3.1 Keyword scorecard

Score each candidate from 0–2 in the following areas. Prioritize pages scoring 7 or more out of 10.

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business fit | Service is not offered | Possible fit | Confirmed core service | Service owner approval |
| Local intent | Informational/national intent | Mixed | Clear city/service search | Search query and SERP |
| Demand | No visible demand | Some impressions or tool volume | Consistent impressions or meaningful volume | Google Search Console and keyword tool |
| Ranking gap | Top results are strong specialist brands | Mixed results | Weak, outdated, or poorly matched results | Incognito SERP review |
| Conversion intent | Research only | Mixed | Ready-to-hire terms such as services, accountant, cleanup, payroll | SERP and lead history |

### 3.2 Required validation process

For every candidate keyword:

1. Search the exact phrase in Google from Texas or use a Texas location setting.
2. Record the first-page result types: local pack, directories, dedicated firms, articles, or government resources.
3. Check Google Search Console for related queries, impressions, average position, and the page that currently receives the impressions.
4. Check a keyword tool available to the team (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, or similar) for location-specific volume and difficulty. Record the date and tool.
5. Prefer a long-tail service + city phrase when broad city terms are dominated by strong local brands or directories.
6. Confirm that the phrase fits a service IntegraFin delivers today.

## 4. Site Architecture

### 4.1 Page types and their jobs

| Page type | URL pattern | Job | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Texas hub | `/texas-tax-accounting-services` | Explains Texas coverage and routes visitors to key cities/services | Explore services or contact us |
| City hub | `/texas/[city]-tax-accountant` | Broad local entry page for business tax and accounting needs | Book a consultation |
| City-service page | `/texas/[city]-[service]` | Answers one high-intent local service need | Request a service review |
| Industry-service hub | `/[service]` | Explains the service across Texas and links to city pages | Discuss the service |
| Resource/article | `/blog/[topic]` | Captures educational intent and supports commercial pages | Read related service page |

### 4.2 Internal-linking rules

Each city-service page must link to:

- Its city hub.
- The Texas services hub.
- One relevant core service page, such as `/quickbooks-bookkeeping-services`, `/bookkeeping-cleanup`, `/business-tax-accounting`, or `/payroll-tax-support`.
- Two relevant nearby city or same-cluster service pages, where they exist.
- The contact page with a descriptive CTA.

Each city hub should link to its active city-service pages in a visible “Services for [City] businesses” section. Do not hide the links only in footer navigation.

## 7. City-Service Page Blueprint

Use this framework for every new city-service page. Aim for complete, scannable content; word count is a result of usefulness, not a target.

### SEO fields

| Field | Required format | Dallas contractor example |
|---|---|---|
| URL | City + specific service, lowercase, hyphenated | `/texas/dallas-contractor-bookkeeping-services` |
| Title tag | Primary service + city – brand | `Contractor Bookkeeping Services in Dallas, TX | IntegraFin` |
| Meta description | Service, audience, outcome, CTA; no guarantees | `Contractor bookkeeping for Dallas builders and trade businesses. Get organized records, job-costing support, and tax-ready financials from IntegraFin.` |
| H1 | Exact service + city | `Contractor Bookkeeping Services in Dallas, TX` |
| Canonical | Exact public URL | The page’s own URL |

### Content sections

1. **Direct answer / hero:** State the service, city served, business type, and CTA. Be clear whether the service is delivered remotely or from the Katy office.
2. **Who this is for:** List relevant contractors and trade businesses only.
3. **What IntegraFin helps with:** Explain real tasks such as reconciliations, expense categorization, cleanup, financial reports, and tax-ready handoff. Add job-costing, 1099, payroll, sales-tax, retainage, or progress-billing support only when truly offered.
4. **Common scenarios:** Use service-specific situations, not invented client stories.
5. **Process:** Discovery, records review, proposed scope, onboarding, ongoing work, and client communication.
6. **Why this city page exists:** Natural city context with no false office claim. Mention service-area coverage accurately.
7. **FAQs:** Answer four to six questions that reflect the page’s commercial intent.
8. **Related services and cities:** Add internal links with natural anchor text.
9. **CTA:** Invite the visitor to discuss their records and service needs. Do not promise an outcome.

### Required FAQ topics for a contractor page

- What bookkeeping records should a contractor keep?
- Can IntegraFin help clean up bookkeeping before business-tax preparation?
- Can contractor expenses be organized by job or project?
- What records are useful for subcontractor and 1099 reporting?
- Does IntegraFin serve Dallas businesses remotely or from the Katy office?

## 8. Local SEO Quality Controls

### On-page

- Unique title, meta description, H1, introduction, FAQs, and image alt text.
- City used naturally in the title, H1, opening copy, CTA, and one or two contextual sections. Never stuff it repeatedly.
- Service details match the actual engagement scope.
- Clear contact details and accurate service-area language.
- Helpful supporting headings; do not write headings only for keyword repetition.

### Technical

- Page returns `200` and has a self-referencing canonical.
- Page is included in `src/app/sitemap.ts`.
- Page is linked from the Texas hub and at least one relevant local or service page.
- Structured data is valid and matches visible page content. Use `Service`, `FAQPage` when FAQs are visible, and the organization’s real business information only.
- Mobile layout, page speed, headings, and CTA work before publishing.

### Trust and compliance

- Do not manufacture testimonials, reviews, case studies, addresses, or city offices.
- Keep licensure wording accurate.
- Add a tax-information disclaimer where appropriate.
- Have a service owner review all claims about industry knowledge and technical tax/accounting work.

## 9. Publishing Workflow

### Before writing

- [ ] Confirm the service is offered.
- [ ] Complete the keyword scorecard.
- [ ] Check for a competing existing URL or keyword cannibalization risk.
- [ ] Decide the exact primary keyword, user intent, CTA, and internal-link targets.
- [ ] Gather approved local facts and service details.

### While building

- [ ] Create the page with the shared local-page template.
- [ ] Write unique, service-specific copy.
- [ ] Add title, description, H1, FAQs, and schema.
- [ ] Add the page to the sitemap.
- [ ] Add city hub and service hub links in both directions.
- [ ] Add two relevant contextual internal links.

### Pre-publish review

- [ ] Confirm no false local-office claim.
- [ ] Confirm no unlicensed `CPA` language.
- [ ] Check keyword usage reads naturally.
- [ ] Test links, canonical, mobile layout, and form/CTA.
- [ ] Run the project type check and relevant build checks.
- [ ] Record the publish date and primary keyword.

### After publishing

- [ ] Deploy the page.
- [ ] Request indexing in Google Search Console.
- [ ] Check indexing within 7–14 days.
- [ ] Review queries, impressions, clicks, average position, and leads after 28 days.
- [ ] Update the page based on actual queries before adding a near-identical sibling page.
