## 7) On-Page SEO Checklist (Per URL)

- Title tag includes service + location + trust modifier.
- Meta description includes CTA and primary intent.
- Single H1 with exact topical relevance.
- H2/H3 structure answers key user questions.
- Intro gives direct answer in first 2-3 lines.
- Internal links to relevant service and state pages.
- External citations to authoritative tax sources where useful.
- FAQ section targets long-tail questions.
- Image alt text is descriptive and contextual.
- CTA visible above fold and end of page.

6. Update ranking tracker and lead tracker.
7. Execute backlink/citation outreach.
8. Log wins, blockers, and next actions.
