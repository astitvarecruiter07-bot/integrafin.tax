## Initial decision rules

- High sessions but few calculator completions: tighten the post promise or send traffic directly to the calculator.
- Calculator completions but no contact actions: strengthen the calculator's professional-review call to action.
- Low sessions: improve the opening line, reshare from a personal profile, and publish the Day 3 educational variation.
- One channel clearly outperforms: reuse its message and destination while changing `utm_content` for each new creative.
- Do not judge organic search performance from this social campaign alone; review Google Search Console impressions and queries separately after at least seven days.

## Compliance checks before publishing

- Do not promise a refund, penalty avoidance, IRS acceptance, or a guaranteed tax result.
- Keep “first-pass federal estimate” and “planning only” language in promotional copy.
- Do not describe IntegraFin as affiliated with or endorsed by the IRS.
