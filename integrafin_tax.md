1. Executive growth diagnosis

Bottom line

IntegraFin appears to be an early-stage, newly formalized US-facing tax and accounting practice with a credible service breadth but limited externally visible proof. The fastest path to growth is to productize a narrow front-door offer, publish verifiable proof, and run two focused acquisition motions in parallel:

Local demand: Katy/Fort Bend business owners with behind books, QuickBooks cleanup needs, tax deadlines, or IRS notices.

B2B capacity: US CPA and bookkeeping firms that need dependable white-label cleanup, bookkeeping, and tax-production capacity.

The current website already contains many useful assets: local service pages, state pages, a pricing/scope guide, a blog, tax calculators, a contact workflow, and illustrative engagement scenarios. The main gap is not lack of pages; it is lack of evidence that reduces buyer risk: named professionals, credentials, genuine testimonials, completed case studies, quantified process standards, clear package anchors, review volume, security proof, and a visible reason to choose IntegraFin over a local CPA, freelancer, or offshore staffing vendor.

1. SEO and visibility strategy

Google's own guidance says Google generally discovers sites automatically, but Search Console should be used to verify indexing and performance; Google also documents LocalBusiness structured data as a way to communicate hours, business details, and other local information. Sources: Google: Get your website on Google, Search Console performance, LocalBusiness structured data.

Technical SEO checklist

Verify Google Search Console ownership, index coverage, manual actions, Core Web Vitals, sitemap, and page-level queries.

Generate/validate XML sitemap and robots.txt; submit the sitemap.

Audit canonical tags, redirects, trailing-slash consistency, 404s, pagination, and duplicate city/state pages.

Add Organization, LocalBusiness, ProfessionalService, Service, FAQPage where eligible, BreadcrumbList, and Person/author schema; do not fabricate reviews or credentials.

Add author/reviewer blocks, last-reviewed dates, citations to primary sources, and a correction process on tax content.

Improve internal links: every blog post → service → assessment CTA; every location page → relevant service → proof.

Use descriptive titles/meta, short URLs, one clear H1, unique copy, accessible images, descriptive alt text, and fast mobile layouts.

Create separate canonical pages for local intent and national/remote service intent; do not mass-produce near-duplicate doorway pages.

1. Retention, referrals, and expansion

Client journey

Signed scope → secure access and records checklist → kickoff → baseline and open-question list → first close/cleanup summary → 30-day check-in → monthly close → quarterly review → tax readiness → renewal/expansion → review/referral request.

Retention system

Day 0: signed scope, owner, communication channel, response expectations.

Day 7: access and record completeness check.

Day 14–30: baseline report and first visible win.

Monthly: close package, unresolved items, next deadline.

Quarterly: review KPIs, scope fit, client satisfaction, and upcoming tax/payroll risks.

Annual: renewal, price/scope review, tax return readiness, referral/testimonial ask.

Health score

Green: on-time records, no unresolved critical items, client replies, payment current, satisfaction ≥8/10.
Amber: repeated late records, scope creep, missed close, payment friction, low engagement.
Red: unresolved tax/payroll deadline, data-security concern, complaint, repeated rework, churn signal.

Referral program

Prefer a non-cash, relationship-safe program: thank-you note, educational session, or charitable contribution where legally and professionally appropriate. Do not pay a referral fee or share professional fees without counsel/board/credential-holder review. Ask after a measurable service win, with no pressure and no review gating. Use: “If you know an owner or firm that needs cleaner books or more capacity, an introduction is appreciated. We will first assess fit and will not disclose your information without permission.”
