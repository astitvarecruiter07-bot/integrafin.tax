# IntegraFin 30-Day Full SEO, AEO, Local and Growth SOP

**Execution period:** August 10–September 8, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Business:** IntegraFin Tax & Accounting  
**Website:** https://integrafin.tax  
**SOP owner:** Managing Director  
**Operational tracker:** `seo-track.md`  
**Next review:** September 8, 2026

> This is the authoritative first-30-day execution plan. It merges the current growth audit, the 90-day growth SOP, the Google/SEO/AEO plan, the Texas SEO SOP, the page rulebook, the repository’s current SEO tracker, and current official Google guidance. When an older plan conflicts with this SOP, this SOP controls.

> “Fully optimized” means the site is technically accessible, clearly mapped to search intent, trustworthy, useful, conversion-ready, measured through the CRM, and supported by a repeatable operating process. It does not mean guaranteed rankings, traffic, leads, tax outcomes, or rich results.

## 1. Thirty-day objective

Build one measurable organic growth system around this path:

`Google/AI/local discovery → high-intent page → Books-to-Tax-Ready Assessment → FITS qualification → written scope → Cleanup Sprint or Monthly Close → retained client/review/referral`

Run a second, smaller B2B path:

`CPA-firm search/LinkedIn/partner discovery → CPA Capacity Audit → qualified 30-day pilot → recurring capacity relationship`

At day 30, IntegraFin must be able to answer:

- Which queries and pages produce qualified prospects?
- Which sources produce assessments, proposals, clients, revenue, and gross profit?
- Which pages are indexed, excluded, underperforming, or competing with one another?
- Which trust, proof, credential, security, or service claims are verified?
- Can the team respond to and deliver new work without weakening quality?

## 3. Superseded instructions from older roadmaps

Do not execute the following older recommendations:

- Do not publish accuracy, maximum-refund, timely-filing, ranking, savings, penalty, or IRS-outcome guarantees.
- Do not display CPA, CA, EA, PTIN, EFIN, certification, association, award, security, or trust badges unless the exact claim is verified and approved.
- Do not use invented testimonial results, stock identities, fake ratings, or review gating.
- Do not create a testimonial slider from unverified statements.
- Do not create duplicate or keyword-stuffed Google Business Profiles, names, categories, or service areas.
- Do not claim offices in cities where IntegraFin has no eligible, staffed location.
- Do not mass-create directory listings merely to increase citation count.
- Do not publish three long articles solely to satisfy a word-count quota.
- Do not add more state or city pages during this 30-day cycle.
- Do not optimize to raw traffic, arbitrary review counts, or assumed session targets.

## 9. Daily operating standards during all 30 days

### Lead response

1. Create/update the CRM record immediately.
2. Record source, landing page, segment, requested service, and consent/suppression status.
3. Assign an owner.
4. Respond within 15 business minutes during published hours.
5. Do not request sensitive records through public forms, email, analytics, or chat.
6. Use FITS: Fit, Issue, Timing, Scope/economics.
7. Leave every record with a dated next action.

### Content and tax accuracy

- Use official primary sources for tax/regulatory facts where available.
- A credentialed reviewer approves tax claims and review dates.
- Explain that outcomes depend on facts, records, eligibility, elections, timing, law, and engagement scope.
- Distinguish bookkeeping, tax preparation, notice review, resolution/representation, payroll work, and advisory.
- Never change a date merely to make content appear fresh.

### Security

- Public forms collect only basic contact/qualification information.
- Sensitive documents use the approved portal.
- MFA, unique accounts, least privilege, access removal, backups, vendor review, WISP, and incident response are mandatory controls.
- A security concern is an immediate stop-and-escalate event.

## 11. Page writing and optimization standard

Each commercial or local page should include, as appropriate:

1. Unique title, description, canonical, and one H1.
2. Direct answer identifying service, audience, and location/model.
3. Problem situations the page addresses.
4. Included work, excluded work, records, dependencies, and timing.
5. Process and named accountability/review.
6. Real proof or clearly labeled illustrative scenarios.
7. Local/state context only where useful and accurate.
8. Five to eight real FAQs when they improve the page.
9. Descriptive internal links to the intent owner, supporting resources, and next step.
10. Visible CTA appropriate to urgency and service.
11. Author/reviewer and substantive reviewed date for tax/YMYL content.
12. Schema that matches visible content.

No minimum word count overrides clarity, originality, or usefulness.

## 12. Content publish/no-publish gate

A page may publish only if every applicable item is true:

- [ ] One primary intent and one owner URL.
- [ ] Original, useful content for a real audience.
- [ ] Verified factual, credential, service, price, location, and outcome language.
- [ ] Credentialed review completed where required.
- [ ] Sensitive/client facts removed or separately permissioned.
- [ ] Title, description, H1, canonical, robots, and sitemap are correct.
- [ ] Internal links and CTA are useful and functional.
- [ ] Schema matches visible content.
- [ ] Mobile layout and accessibility pass.
- [ ] Analytics contains no sensitive data.
- [ ] Lead path, CRM attribution, and notification pass when applicable.
- [ ] Human grammar/readability review completed.

## 14. KPI dashboard

### Search visibility

- Non-branded clicks and impressions.
- CTR and average position by query/page/device.
- Indexed versus submitted pages.
- Exclusion reasons and crawl errors.
- Local service queries and correct landing-page ownership.
- Core Web Vitals field status.
- Manual actions and security issues.

### Conversion and CRM

- Organic landing sessions.
- CTA clicks, form starts, lead submissions, phone/WhatsApp clicks, and booking completions.
- Qualified leads meeting FITS.
- Speed-to-lead; initial target under 15 business minutes in hours.
- Held assessments / booked; initial target at least 75%.
- Proposals / qualified opportunities; initial target 50–70%.
- Wins / proposals; initial target 25–40% after sufficient volume.
- Revenue and gross profit by source, landing page, service, and segment.
- Lost reason and sales cycle.

### Delivery and trust

- Available capacity by service/reviewer.
- On-time delivery; initial target at least 95%.
- Material rework; initial target below 5%.
- Client health: green, amber, red.
- Permissioned proof assets, neutral review requests, and resulting reviews.
- Security, credential, complaint, or scope exceptions.

Treat early percentage targets as planning assumptions until the sample is large enough to interpret.

## 15. Stop rules

Pause work immediately when:

- A tax, credential, licensing, privacy, security, location, review, referral, or unsupported-claim risk appears.
- A page would duplicate an existing search intent.
- A new city/state page has no evidence of distinct demand or unique value.
- Outreach produces material deliverability or compliance concerns.
- Response time or delivery quality falls below the agreed standard.
- A campaign cannot connect spend to qualified opportunities and gross profit.
- A package is unprofitable after delivery, review, software, rework, and acquisition cost.

After two documented learning cycles dominated by poor-fit leads, stop or redesign the channel.
